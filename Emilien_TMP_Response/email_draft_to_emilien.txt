Since the coefficients are complex, it would be best to plot the exact quantities used to train the GP. For example, if you train on real and imaginary parts, plot true vs GP for Re(q_i) and Im(q_i), or at least one of them first. If you train on modulus/phase, then plot those quantities instead.

Then I think we will be able to discuss the GP failure much more concretely.
